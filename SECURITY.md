# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately, not in public issues.

- Preferred: GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button under this repository's **Security** tab.
- Or email **info@kallam.ai** with "security" in the subject.

Include the affected version or commit, steps to reproduce and the impact you see. We'll acknowledge reports within 3 business days and keep you updated until a fix ships. Fixes are released on the latest version only.
