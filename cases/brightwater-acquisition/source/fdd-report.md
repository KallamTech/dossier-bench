# FINANCIAL DUE DILIGENCE REPORT

**Target:** Brightwater Analytics Ltd ("Brightwater" or the "Company")

**Prepared for:** Castellane Holdings Ltd (the "Buyer")

**Prepared by:** Hollis & Marr LLP, Transaction Services — Eleanor Marr, Partner

**Date:** 28 February 2025

**Project:** Larch

Private and confidential. This report is addressed to the Buyer only and is subject to our engagement letter dated 20 January 2025. We have relied on management information in the Data Room and on discussions with Imogen Hale (CEO), Rafael Duarte (CTO) and Martin Keogh (Finance Manager). We have not audited the information.

## 1. Executive summary

1.1 Brightwater provides a subscription analytics platform to 41 UK mid-market customers in retail, utilities, logistics and the public sector. Annual recurring revenue ("ARR") was £5.9m at 31 December 2024 (the proposed Locked Box Date), up from £4.8m at 31 December 2023 (growth of 23%).

1.2 Normalised EBITDA for FY2024 was £1.45m (24.6% margin) after adjustments of £0.21m set out in section 3.

1.3 Net cash at the Locked Box Date was £1.2m. There is no bank debt. Working capital is seasonal because 60% of customers are invoiced annually in advance in January.

1.4 The indicative price of £18.5m in the heads of terms of 17 January 2025 represents 3.1x ARR and 12.8x normalised EBITDA.

## 2. Key findings

## 3. Quality of earnings

3.1 Reported EBITDA FY2024: £1.24m. Adjustments: add back one-off recruitment fees £0.09m; add back Project Larch advisory costs £0.12m; deduct capitalised development expensed (£0.60m) and add back amortisation of previously capitalised costs £0.60m. Normalised EBITDA £1.45m.

## 4. Revenue and ARR

4.1 ARR bridge FY2024: opening £4.8m; new logos +£0.9m; upsell +£0.6m; churn and downsell (£0.4m); closing £5.9m.

4.2 Top ten customers represent 80% of ARR. Northgate Retail plc (£1.30m, 22%) is the only customer above 10%. Avalon Pharmacies (£0.62m), Calder Vale Water (£0.54m), Mirren Logistics Group (£0.47m) and Hadley Grocery Co-operative (£0.41m) follow. We flag the Northgate renewal as the key risk to the investment case: the master services agreement was renewed for 12 months only in July 2024, which management attributes to Northgate's procurement policy.

4.3 Two contracts were signed in Q4 2024 with go-live in 2025 (not included in ARR): Caldera Energy expansion and a public-sector pilot.

## 5. Net cash and debt-like items

5.1 Cash £1.35m less accrued bonuses £0.15m = net cash £1.2m. Debt-like items for consideration: deferred revenue £2.1m, accrued holiday pay £0.08m, corporation tax payable £0.11m.

## 6. Working capital

6.1 Average trade working capital over the last twelve months was negative £1.4m, reflecting advance invoicing. We recommend a locked-box mechanism rather than a completion accounts adjustment.

## 7. Employment

7.1 Headcount 48 at 31 December 2024 (engineering 21, customer success 9, sales 8, other 10). Management confirmed that no claims are pending or threatened; one informal grievance in the customer success team was described as resolved.

## 8. Limitations

8.1 This report does not cover legal, tax or technical due diligence, which are being carried out by Fenwick Rowe LLP, Hollis & Marr Tax and an external technical adviser respectively.
