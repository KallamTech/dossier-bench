# PROJECT KITE — LAUNCH BRIEF

**Product:** Kite Planner 3.0

**Owner:** Nadia Rahimi, Product Manager — Larkspur Labs Ltd

**Date:** 4 July 2025

**Status:** approved by the leadership team on 7 July 2025

## 1. Why

Kite Planner 2.x is used by 410 customers. The three most requested changes over the last year are a faster dashboard with saved views, single sign-on for enterprise customers, and scheduled report exports. Version 3.0 delivers all three and is the basis for the enterprise tier planned for 2026.

## 2. Goals

- Launch on **15 October 2025**.
- 25% of existing customers using 3.0 within 90 days of launch.
- Beta net promoter score (NPS) of at least 40 across about 12 beta customers.
- Launch within the budget of **£120,000** (video, paid media, event, beta incentives, design support, contingency).
