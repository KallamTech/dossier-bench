# FLOOR SLAB LEVEL SURVEY — BAY 3

**Levantina Topografía SL** — Calle del Faro Viejo 211, 46023 Valencia — CIF B-00.000.001

**Client:** Tidewell Fabrication Ltd (attention Tomasz Wójcik)

**Site:** Brisamar Logistics distribution centre, Polígono Industrial Los Almendros, Paterna

**Survey date:** 19 February 2025 | **Report reference:** LT-2025-0218 | **Issued:** 19 February 2025

## 1. Scope

1.1 Tidewell Fabrication Ltd instructed Levantina Topografía SL to survey the finished floor level of Bay 3 of the installation hall along the proposed sorter centreline and at the support positions shown on Tidewell drawing HFL-VAL-GA-003 rev C.

1.2 The survey was carried out with a Leica LS15 digital level and a 2 m invar staff on a 1 m grid between grid lines C1 and C10 (sorter length 68 m) and over a 4 m width either side of the centreline.

1.3 Results are reported against the tolerance stated in clause 6.2 of the Supply and Installation Agreement: 5 mm over any 3 m span, measured in accordance with DIN 18202 Table 3 line 4.

## 2. Results

| Zone | Grid lines | Max deviation over 3 m | Within 5 mm tolerance? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | C1–C3 | 3 mm | Yes |
| 2 | C3–C4 | 6 mm | No (marginal) |
| 3 | C4–C7 | **18 mm** | No |
| 4 | C7–C9 | 7 mm | No |
| 5 | C9–C10 | 4 mm | Yes |

2.1 The slab between grid lines C4 and C7 shows a depression with a maximum deviation of 18 mm over 3 m, centred approximately on C5. This exceeds the 5 mm contract tolerance by 13 mm and exceeds the ±20 mm adjustment range of the sorter supports when combined with the adjacent high point at C7.

2.2 Zones 2 and 4 are marginally outside tolerance and could be accommodated by support adjustment and local grouting.

## 3. Conclusions

3.1 The floor slab in Bay 3 between grid lines C4 and C7 does not comply with the 5 mm over 3 m tolerance in clause 6.2 of the contract.

3.2 In our opinion the sorter cannot be installed to the specified alignment (±2 mm) in Zone 3 without re-levelling the slab, either by grinding the high points at C4 and C7 and applying a self-levelling screed in the depression, or by installing a steel sub-frame grouted to the slab.

3.3 Re-levelling by screed would require a minimum of seven days for application and curing before supports can be fixed.
