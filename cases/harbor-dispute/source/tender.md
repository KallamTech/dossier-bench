## 2. Understanding of the requirement

2.1 Brisamar requires a system to receive inbound cartons from four dock doors, merge them onto a single sorter and divert them to 26 outbound lanes according to the destination encoded in the GS1-128 shipping label. The sustained design throughput is 4,800 cartons per hour with a peak of 5,400 cartons per hour for periods of up to thirty minutes.

2.2 The installation hall is a new build with a power-floated concrete slab. The site information pack states a floor flatness of 5 mm over 3 m. Our conveyor supports are adjustable by ±20 mm and can accommodate minor deviations; larger deviations would require local grouting or re-levelling by the Buyer.

2.3 The hall will be served by a 400A three-phase supply from the Buyer's main switchboard. Our controls design assumes this supply is available at the installation hall distribution board before commissioning starts.

2.4 Integration with the Buyer's warehouse management system (Manhattan SCALE) will be over OPC UA, with the sort destination table downloaded hourly and exception reporting in real time.

## 3. Technical proposal

3.1 **Receiving lines.** Four 24 m powered roller lines with zero-pressure accumulation, 620 mm between frames, 0.8 m/s nominal.

3.2 **Merge.** A 4-to-1 sawtooth merge with gapping belts, controlled by the sorter PLC to maintain a 400 mm minimum gap at the induct.

3.3 **Sorter.** A 68 m narrow-belt sorter with pop-up wheel diverts at 26 positions. The sorter is modular in 3 m sections for ease of transport and installation.

3.4 **Scanning.** Two five-sided camera tunnels (Cognex) with a guaranteed 99.5% read rate on compliant labels.

3.5 **Controls.** Siemens S7-1500 PLC, ET200SP distributed I/O, two 15-inch HMI stations and a SCADA server. PLC programming, HMI and SCADA integration will be performed by our controls partner **Velden Controls BV** of Eindhoven, who have worked with us on every sorter installation since 2019.

3.6 **Safety.** Perimeter fencing to EN ISO 14120, light curtains at manual induct stations, emergency stop circuits designed and validated to PL d.
