# ANNEX A — TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION

**Supply and Installation Agreement dated 4 November 2024 — Project NWL-VAL-2024-07**

**Automated Conveyor and Sortation System, Valencia Distribution Centre**

Revision B — issued with Amendment No. 1 on 20 January 2025

## 1. Scope

1.1 This Annex A sets out the performance, design, supply, installation and commissioning requirements for the automated conveyor and sortation system (the "System") to be supplied and installed by Tidewell Fabrication Ltd (the "Supplier") at Brisamar Logistics SA's Valencia Distribution Centre at Polígono Industrial Los Almendros, Paterna (the "Site").

1.2 In the event of conflict between this Annex and the body of the Agreement, the body of the Agreement prevails.

## 2. Performance requirements

2.1 Design throughput: the sorter shall process not less than 6,000 parcels per hour sustained over any 60-minute period, with a peak of 7,200 parcels per hour for 15 minutes.

2.2 Sortation accuracy: not less than 99.7% of parcels delivered to the correct outfeed lane, measured over 10,000 consecutive parcels.

2.3 Parcel envelope: 100 x 100 x 30 mm minimum to 800 x 600 x 500 mm maximum; mass 0.1 kg to 30 kg.

2.4 Availability: not less than 98.5% during operating hours, excluding planned maintenance.

## 3. Site conditions to be provided by the Buyer

3.1 Floor slab: level to a tolerance of 5 mm over any 3 m span measured in accordance with DIN 18202 Table 3, line 4, in all installation areas (see clause 6.2 of the Agreement).

3.2 Electrical supply: a permanent 400A three-phase 400 V 50 Hz supply terminated at the main control panel in Bay 1 (see clause 6.4 as introduced by Amendment No. 1), available by 7 February 2025.

3.3 Ambient: 5 °C to 40 °C, non-condensing; the Site to be weather-tight before installation starts.

3.4 Network: two fibre pairs from the Site IT room to the main control panel and to the sorter control cabinet in Bay 3.

## 4. System architecture

4.1 Cross-belt sorter, 26 outfeed lanes (lanes 1–26), two inductions, recirculation loop with a second sorter outfeed added under Change Order CO-1.

4.2 Control system: Siemens S7-1500 redundant PLC pair (Change Order CO-2), Profinet field network, WinCC Unified HMI at the Bay 1 main control panel.

4.3 Warehouse control software: Supplier's SortMaster 4 with the interface to the Buyer's WMS specified in section 9.

## 5. Mechanical design

5.1 Conveyor supports adjustable within ±20 mm of nominal height; the sorter carriage track in Bay 3 to be set within ±1.5 mm across its length.

5.2 Guarding to EN ISO 13857; safety fencing at outfeed lanes 20–26 per Change Order CO-4.

5.3 Belt conveyors: 600 mm and 800 mm belt widths, variable speed 0.5 to 2.5 m/s.
